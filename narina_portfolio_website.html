Design Philosophy

Reducing friction, respecting attention, and empowering every human touchpoint.

As a Human-Centered Design student and UX Engineer, my work centers on the principle that digital and physical tools should dynamically adapt to human limits—not force humans to adapt to technology.

Combining Nielsen’s classic usability heuristics with cognitive ergonomics, I design predictive gaming overlays that prevent information overload during fast-paced combat, develop hands-free footwear entries for mobility constraints, and design screen-free haptic feedback systems that preserve mental focus.
